ABSTRACT

Phishing is one of the most critical cybersecurity threats today, severely exacerbated by AI-crafted and zero-day campaigns that evade static rule-based filters. Traditional detection approaches rely on a single data source—such as URL attributes or email body text—leaving significant blind spots. To resolve this limitation, we present an Adaptive Multi-Modal Phishing Detection Framework combining deep learning and real-time user behavioural analytics. Our architecture integrates a fine-tuned BERT transformer for contextual email text classification, a deep Character-level CNN with 14 structural features for malicious URL detection, and an Isolation Forest algorithm for identifying abnormal browsing dynamics. Individual predictions are fused through an intelligent multi-tier risk engine, eliminating single-point vulnerabilities and delivering robust, zero-day threat protection with 99.3% accuracy.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

  • Rise of AI-Generated Phishing: Attackers routinely exploit Large Language Models to craft grammatically flawless, contextually authentic zero-day lures without static signatures.
  • Single-Source Blind Spots: Existing solutions analyze URLs or text in isolation; benign-looking emails containing stealthy links or clean URLs on compromised hosts slip through.
  • Neglect of User Behavioural Cues: Traditional systems completely ignore post-click interactions (excessive redirects, abnormal mouse velocity, multiple failed login attempts).
  • Fragility of Static Blacklists: Rule-based and whitelist techniques require continuous manual updating, degrade rapidly against new domains, and trigger frequent false positives.

OBJECTIVES

  • 01
    Email Semantic Detection: Train and fine-tune transformer-based BERT to capture contextual semantics and coercive social-engineering language.
  • 02
    Character-Level URL Analysis: Develop a deep Character-Level CNN with 14 lexical & structural attributes to catch obfuscated zero-day URLs.
  • 03
    Behavioural Anomaly Scoring: Implement an Isolation Forest engine to flag post-click anomalies across 8 user interaction features in real time.
  • 04
    Adaptive Multi-Modal Fusion: Synthesize multi-source signals via an intelligent weighting scheme to produce a calibrated, explainable risk score.

CONCLUSION

  • Eliminates Single-Vector Blind Spots: Concurrently evaluating linguistic content, URL patterns, and user telemetry reliably defeats sophisticated multi-stage attacks.
  • Proven State-of-the-Art Accuracy: BERT achieved 99.33% accuracy on emails while CNN reached 99.51% ROC-AUC on URLs, confirming deep architectures outperform classical ML.
  • Behavioural Safety Net: Isolation Forest adds an invaluable post-click layer, flagging rapid redirects and abnormal interactions even when content appears benign.
